How do you turn Tesla dashcam clips into 3D? [pause]
Step one. Copy the TeslaCam folder off the USB drive in your glovebox.
Step two. Pick twenty seconds of driving. We pull frames from every camera, and mask out your own car.
Step three. COLMAP solves all the cameras together as one rigid rig, and Brush trains a Gaussian splat on your Mac's GPU.
Step four. Open the replay: a chase cam behind your car, with every raw camera feed beside it.
It all runs locally, and the code is on GitHub.
